# MERC ZEBRA

**Play it in your browser: <https://anderspartnersck.github.io/MERCZEBRA/>**

A May-gray delivery run across the Valley. You're Mercury. Keep moving.

**Status:** playable, not finished. All 11 campaign stages play, with heat and cops; the city dressing,
drop-off density and difficulty past the mid-game are still being worked. It's a big download (about 120 MB).

## About

Top-down delivery driving through a real-feeling San Fernando Valley: the 101, its on-ramps and its jams,
named drop-offs, cops that warm up as you push. The campaign runs from Northridge through the Valley, out to
Santa Clarita and Ventura, and ends in the Mojave. The rule the whole game is built around is **KEEP MOVING**.

## How to play

| | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Steer | **← / →** | stick or D-pad |
| Gas | **↑**, **Z** or **A** | **A** |
| Brake | **↓**, **X** or **S** | **B** |
| Ride (pick up / drop off, start, confirm) | **SPACE** or **ENTER** | **Y** |
| Lay low (bleed heat, shake the cops) | **SHIFT** or **C** | **X** |
| Horn (the gallop: leapfrog traffic) | **H** | **LB** |
| Pause | **ESC** or **P** | **SELECT** |

The pause menu has a HOW TO DRIVE card with the same list.

**On a phone,** turn it sideways: a steering pad and six buttons (GAS, BRAKE, RIDE, LAY LOW, HORN, START) sit
beside the game. The ⇄ LEFTY chip mirrors the two sides.

## Stage select

The main link is the **coin-op campaign**: one credit, stage 1, no skipping. For the **STAGE SELECT**, open
the jailbroken MAY GRAY cab:

**[MERC ZEBRA — JAILBREAK / STAGE SELECT](https://anderspartnersck.github.io/MERCZEBRA/?cab=maygray)**

Gas/brake (or click) to choose, RIDE to go, LAY LOW to back out. It lists RUN ALL (the whole campaign on one
credit), every stage on its own, including **THE COURTESY**, the DTLA bonus level the campaign never reaches,
plus the cheat panel. Runs started from here **do not post to the board**.
